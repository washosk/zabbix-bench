🚀 NEXT STEPS FOR USERS

1. Install:
   gh release download v1.0.0 -p "zabbix-bench*" -D ~/bin/
   
2. Test:
   ./zabbix-bench -api-url "http://localhost/zabbix/api_jsonrpc.php" \
                  -hosts 10 -duration 30s
   
3. Configure:
   Create benchmark.yaml with your settings
   
4. Run:
   ./zabbix-bench -config benchmark.yaml -output-json results.json
   
5. Analyze:
   cat results.json | jq '.p95_latency_ms, .throughput_vps'
